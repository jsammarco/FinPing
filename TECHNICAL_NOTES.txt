FinPing technical notes

Current implementation status: TX only. RF reception and DTMF decoding are planned
future work and are not present in this build.

Core idea:
  PCM WAV preamble or DTMF sine pair -> pulse-density modulation -> CC1101 async 2-FSK input

WAV support:
  Mono or stereo, uncompressed PCM only; 8-bit unsigned or 16-bit signed,
  with 8-48 kHz source sample rates. Stereo is downmixed and source rates above
  16 kHz are downsampled before samples are preloaded into RAM and sent to the
  20 kHz PDM stream. MP3 decoding is intentionally not included.

Message pause tokens:
  P  = 1 second of silence
  LP = 3 seconds of silence

Sample rate:
  20 kHz / 50 us per PDM symbol

CC1101 preset:
  FuriHalSubGhzPreset2FSKDev238Async

Expected behavior:
  CC1101 toggles between carrier +/- ~2.38 kHz.
  The time-average frequency shift follows the DTMF waveform.
  A normal FM discriminator followed by receiver low-pass filtering should reconstruct
  the two DTMF tones sufficiently for some decoders.

This is intentionally not implemented as OOK RAW data: OOK would amplitude-key the
carrier rather than approximate frequency modulation.

Potential next experiments if decode is marginal:
  1. Custom CC1101 async preset with a higher configured data rate.
  2. 24/32/40 ksample/s PDM.
  3. Second-order sigma-delta instead of first-order pulse-density accumulation.
  4. Per-tone twist adjustment (high group -2 dB).
  5. Soft attack/release envelopes to reduce clicks.
  6. Calibration screen that repeatedly transmits a chosen digit.
  7. Scope/test-tone modes: 697, 770, 852, 941, 1209, 1336, 1477, 1633 Hz.
